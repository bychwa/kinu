# Acme Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing
at roughly 2.08 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the
following year and must be used by March 31. Requests longer than 10
consecutive days require two weeks notice to your manager.

## Expenses

Expense reports are due within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Receipts
are required for anything over $25. Travel must be booked through the corporate
tool; exceptions require written approval from a director. Reimbursement
typically lands in the next payroll cycle.

## Security

All laptops must have full disk encryption enabled and screen lock set to five
minutes or less. Company data may only be stored in approved systems. Uploading
customer data to unapproved third-party services is a reportable incident.
Security incidents must be reported to security@ within one hour of discovery.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements are considered case by case and require a written
agreement covering working hours and equipment.
